HQ

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who uses HQ

HQ is a personal dashboard used only by its owner, on their own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What HQ accesses

When the owner connects a Google account, HQ asks Google for permission to see the list of that account's calendars and to read, create, change and delete events on them, so they can be shown and managed in HQ. It also reads the account's email address to label which account is which. HQ does not access Gmail messages, contacts, files or anything else.

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. Calendar data and the sign-in tokens Google issues are kept only on the owner's own computer. HQ has no servers of its own, and it does not sell, share, transfer or use this data for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than showing and managing the owner's own calendar.

HQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Bank accounts

When the owner connects their bank, HQ reads their own accounts through Open Banking, using Enable Banking as the regulated account information provider. With the owner's consent, given at their bank, HQ reads account details, balances and transactions. It cannot make payments. This data is kept only on the owner's own computer and is not shared with anyone or used for any other purpose.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page, or by disconnecting the account inside HQ, which also deletes its stored sign-in. Bank access can be ended at the bank or with Disconnect in HQ, which also deletes the bank data HQ kept.
