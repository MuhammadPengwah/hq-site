What HQ is

HQ is a private dashboard that its owner runs on their own computer, for their own use. It is not a service: it isn't offered, sold or provided to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

Bank accounts

HQ reads the owner's own bank accounts through Open Banking, with the owner's consent given at their bank. Access is read-only: HQ reads account details, balances and transactions to show them to the owner. It does not make payments or move money. The owner can end access at any time, in HQ or at their bank.

No other users

Nobody other than the owner may use HQ or connect accounts to it. HQ comes with no warranty of any kind.
